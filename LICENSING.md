# Licensing

This repository is a lore handbook for the world of *Beyond the Mountains*, by Håkan Arnoldson, and in time a collection of stories set there. This page says what you may do with it. It is written in plain words; the license texts themselves govern.

You may copy, share, adapt and build on it for any purpose, including commercially, as long as you give credit, link the license and say what you changed. The full text is in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE); the summary is at <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>.

For an adapted work, add a note of what you changed.

1. **Code.** Scripts, build tooling, layout and web code (anything under a `tools/` folder, or ending in `.py`, `.js`, `.mjs`, `.html` or `.css`) are under the MIT License in [`LICENSE-CODE`](LICENSE-CODE), so they can be reused in projects that are not Creative Commons licensed.
2. **Third-party material.** Quotations and excerpts from other people's work, such as DreamGen's documentation, community guides, forum and chat posts, or other authors' writing, stay under their owners' terms and are not licensed by us. They are marked where they appear. If one is missing its mark, tell us and it will be fixed or removed.
3. **Names and marks.** DreamGen and other product and company names belong to their owners. Nothing here is endorsed by them, and CC BY 4.0 licenses no trademarks (section 2(b)(2) of the license).
4. **AI-assisted work.** Much of this repository is drafted with AI tools, and some images are generated with them; the author directs, selects and edits. Where a tool's terms limit the reuse of what it produced, the file says so and that limit comes first. Whether machine-made images carry copyright at all varies by country; we license whatever rights we hold in them under CC BY 4.0 and claim no more.
5. **Drafts.** Files marked DRAFT, in the name or in a header, are licensed the same but are unstable: they can be rewritten or withdrawn. If you build on one, cite the commit.
6. **Game files.** Scenario files for DreamGen or another platform, if any are included, are CC BY 4.0 like the rest. On the platform itself, the platform's terms apply to what is uploaded there.
7. **Canon and derivatives.** Derivative works, such as fan fiction, games, maps and translations, are welcome under the license. Canon is what this repository says at a given tag or commit; a derivative must not present itself as canon or as the author's own work. CC BY 4.0 already forbids implying endorsement; this is the plain-language version.
8. **Contributions.** By contributing, you agree that your contribution is licensed under the same terms, CC BY 4.0 for content and MIT for code, and that you have the right to grant that.
