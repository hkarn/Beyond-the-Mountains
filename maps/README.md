# Maps (DRAFT)

Sketch maps of the world, drawn for the draft stage on 2026-11-08. The geography on them is the working reference while the lore is honed, and nothing on them is fixed canon yet: places can move, be renamed or go. The SVG files are the source of truth for geography; everything else is derived from them.

## The Veiled Range and Cinderfell

- `veiled-range-sketch-map.svg`, `.png`: schematic of the Range and the city. The seven Quiet Vales; Hollow Vale (IV) in detail with its three hamlets, the stone ring, the river that goes under the rock and the Scar on its eastern wall; the Ashen Line with its ward-cairns; Blackpost Station three miles above the Scar's foot; the Ashen Road twenty miles down to Cinderfell's tiers; the Sky-Moorings on the bluff; the rails east; the high winds; scale, compass and legend. The PNG is a rendering of the SVG at twice scale.
- `veiled-range-painted-test.webp`: one painted test made from the schematic with an image model (Seedream 5.0 Flash), using `painted-prompt.txt`. Lettering partly garbled; two label collisions that it inherited from the first schematic have since been fixed in the SVG; the airships are drawn as rigid dirigibles where the world has windrow-beasts; Hollow Vale is drawn as a crater.

## The Concord's lands

- `wider-world-sketch-map.svg`, `.png`: schematic of the wider world as described in the gazetteer draft. The Range as a crescent with cliffs to the Shelf Sea; the three towns on the Ashen Line; the aether bands; the Ashwater; the Coalmarch, the Ember Downs and Emberlock, the Lowmeads, Ironmere on the Mere, Saltgate on the Southern Reach; the Tarn Marshes; the high winds.
- `wider-world-painted-test.webp`: painted test from the schematic, same method, using `wider-painted-prompt.txt`.

## Toward a real edition

The painted tests are accepted as drafts, garbled lettering included. A proper edition keeps the SVG as the geographic source and either uses an image model that handles lettering or paints the map unlabelled and lays the lettering over it in the SVG or with HTML and CSS. An in-world chart style is wanted for the handbook, with the Compact's grey, blanked Range drawn as a feature of the chart.
